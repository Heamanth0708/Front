GLOBAL HOME CARE AI MULTI-SERVICE INVOICE & PAYMENT TRACKER V5

MAIN UPDATE
- One customer can receive one combined invoice.
- Add multiple Nursing Service items.
- Add multiple Medical Equipment items.
- Add Nursing Services and Medical Equipment together in the same invoice.
- Each item has separate type, name, description, quantity/days, rate and amount.
- Combined total, GST, discount, amount received and balance are calculated automatically.
- Invoice category and applicable terms are selected automatically.

TERMS
- Nursing-only invoice: Nursing Services terms.
- Equipment-only invoice: Medical Equipment terms.
- Combined invoice: Both Nursing and Medical Equipment terms.

OTHER FEATURES
- AI payment-risk score and reminder priority.
- AI follow-up recommendations.
- AI WhatsApp reminders.
- Automatic due dates.
- Paid, Part Payment, Due and Overdue tracking.
- Paid and pending dashboard.
- Overdue browser notifications.
- Original Global Home Care logo.
- Print and Save PDF.
- Search and filter invoice history.

HOW TO USE
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open Global_Home_Care_AI_Multi_Service_Invoice_Tracker.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Enter customer details.
4. Add every nursing service or equipment item using the Add button.
5. Save the invoice.
6. Print PDF or share through WhatsApp.
